**Favorite Movie**
> Lolo and the Kid

>“Lolo and the Kid” tells the story of a young boy who is raised by his grandfather, whom he lovingly calls Lolo. Despite their simple and sometimes difficult life, the two share a deep bond filled with love, patience, and understanding. Lolo teaches the kid important lessons about kindness, responsibility, and resilience. As the story progresses, the boy begins to understand the sacrifices his grandfather makes for him. In the end, their relationship shows the power of family, love, and the wisdom that comes with age.
